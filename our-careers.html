Careers

Benefits with Purpose

As an employee-owned business, we recognise people are our power. So we pay attention to them. Whether it's the freedom of agile working, being empowered to use your voice or the support offered by our committees and groups, we believe that looking after people isn't just the right thing to do, it makes good business sense.

Family First

There are parents and carers throughout Tangerine in all our agencies, and it's important to us to support you. We are proud to have an inclusive range of family friendly policies to support our team with family life:

  • Enhanced maternity leave & adoption leave
  • Supported return to work for new parents (up to 12 weeks)
  • Parental leave & carer's leave
  • Agile working
  • Time off for appointments incl. fertility / treatment appointments
  • Pension Contributions

Tangerine Fuel

So we're all equipped to go about our day with healthy options and even emergency lunches. Everything you need to fuel your best:

  • Free breakfast & lunch (options available daily)
  • Free healthy snacks and fruit
  • Snack cupboard - all day, every day, as well as a selection of teas and juices

Wellbeing

Physical and mental health are fundamental to our sense of wellbeing. It's not always plain sailing, and it can be hard at times, but we want our team to feel supported and know what tools are available to help them:

  • Wellbeing days (2 per year)
  • Private health insurance
  • Discounted gym membership
  • Menopause training and support
  • Mental health first aiders
  • Regular training on a range of topics e.g. menopause, neurodiversity

When you're off, you're off

When you're not at work, it's very important that you don't think about it. We want you to feel rested and ready to give your best when you're working and we're committed to ensuring that our holidays support this:

  • 25 days annual leave plus bank holidays, rising to 30 days
  • Birthday off (or the closest Monday / Friday if it falls on a weekend)
  • Additional leave purchase - up to 5 extra days to make the most of your weekends
  • Additional leave up to 5 years of service
  • Sabbaticals
  • Bank holiday swaps
  • New home allowance e.g. Mrs Smith and her pups

Giving back

We're serious about putting pounds into pockets at Tangerine. We're proud of the numerous financial benefits we offer and are always looking at how we can give our employees a boost. Our financial wellbeing initiatives include:

  • We benchmark our salaries annually
  • Profit Share - we're employee-owned and so every employee is entitled to a share of profits
  • Matched pension contributions (up to 5%)
  • Interest free loans - up to £5,000
  • Training and development programme funded by us

Shaking it off

Part of what makes us so strong is how close we are, and what better way to get to know your colleagues than by letting your hair down and getting to know each other outside of work? Our social calendar includes a range of shared hobbies and interests to get involved in:

  • Weekly Socials
  • Christmas and Summer Parties
  • Wellness events
  • Social clubs incl. Running Club and Book Club
  • Tangerine FC - Yoga
