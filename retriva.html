Retriva

Meet Retriva

Retriva is Tangerine's visibility tool and framework for measuring and improving brand retrievability in the AI era.

Built on the principles explored in our Unretrievable whitepaper, it helps organisations understand how effectively they are positioned to be discovered, understood and recommended by AI-powered systems.

Retriva helps answer four critical questions
01

How retrievable are we?

02

Which signals need strengthening?

03

How do we compare to our competitors?

04

What should we do next?

Retriva Framework

Our workflow to take you from awareness to action.

Score

Benchmark your retrievability

An initial benchmark of your brand's retrievability relative to competitors.

You'll receive:

  • A Retriva Score
  • Competitive benchmarking
  • Signal strengths
  • Signal gaps
  • Priority opportunities

Audit

Understand what's driving your score

A comprehensive analysis of the signals influencing your AI visibility and retrievability.

Examines:

  • Authority signals
  • Media footprint
  • Social relevance
  • Content ecosystem
  • Competitive positioning
  • GEO performance

Roadmap

Know what to do next

A clear action plan designed to strengthen retrievability over time.

Includes:

  • Prioritised recommendations
  • Content opportunities
  • Authority-building actions
  • Channel-specific improvements
  • Measurement framework

Built for the new era of brand discovery

As AI-powered discovery reshapes how brands are found and recommended, retrievability is influenced by a broad mix of communications signals.

Retriva draws on Tangerine's expertise across earned, social and owned channels to help organisations strengthen the signals that matter most.

From media coverage and social conversation to owned content and third-party endorsement, Tangerine helps brands build the visibility signals AI systems rely upon.
