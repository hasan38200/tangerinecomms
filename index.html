We build Superbrands by making Attention pay

Tangerine is a fiercely independent PR and Social agency. PR Week's Best Agency Outside of London 2025, The Drum's Top 100 Indie Agencies 2025 and Campaign's Global Agency of the Year 2024.

We turn great brands into superbrands for our consumer, B2B and corporate clients. How? Attention.

Our approach is simple. We capture attention, we convert it and we prove it. That's why our clients stick with us, because we deliver results that matter.

PR

Born in PR over 20 years ago, we're the world's leading independent agency, driven by our unwavering commitment to attention. Attention delivers real impact, and our track record proves it. Campaign after campaign, across every sector, we deliver award-winning, game changing results.
